Delicious Chocolate Cake

Moist, simple, and perfect for sharing.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 1/2 cup cocoa powder
  • 1 tsp baking powder
  • 1/2 cup unsalted butter, softened
  • 2 large eggs
  • 1 cup milk

Preparation Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 180°C (350°F).
  2. Whisk together flour, sugar, cocoa, and baking powder.
  3. Add butter, eggs, and milk; mix until smooth.
  4. Pour batter into a greased 9-inch pan.
  5. Bake for 30-35 minutes or until a toothpick comes out clean.
  6. Cool, slice, and serve.
