About PS5

The PlayStation 5 (PS5) is Sony’s latest next-generation gaming console, designed for lightning-fast load times, incredible graphics, and immersive gameplay. It builds upon the success of the PS4, offering cutting-edge technology and a vast library of games for all types of gamers.

  • Ultra-fast SSD for near-instant load times
  • Ray tracing for realistic lighting and shadows
  • 4K gaming with smooth frame rates
  • DualSense controller with haptic feedback
  • Backward compatibility with most PS4 games
