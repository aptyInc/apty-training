Delicious Ramen Recipe

Ingredients

  • 2 packs of ramen noodles
  • 4 cups chicken broth
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon miso paste
  • 1 boiled egg, halved
  • 100g sliced chicken or pork
  • 1 cup chopped green onions
  • 1/2 cup corn kernels
  • 1 sheet nori (seaweed)

Steps

  1. Boil chicken broth in a pot and add soy sauce and miso paste. Stir well.
  2. Add sliced chicken and cook until tender.
  3. Cook the ramen noodles separately as per packet instructions and drain.
  4. Place noodles in a bowl and pour the hot broth with meat over them.
  5. Top with boiled egg halves, green onions, corn, and nori.
  6. Serve hot and enjoy your delicious homemade ramen!
